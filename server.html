    Wipes

    Wipes reset the map, bases and player progress. They are always announced on Discord in advance, so you have time to prepare.

    • Wipe dates are announced at least one week ahead
    • Major DayZ updates may require an extra wipe
    • Bans and staff decisions survive a wipe
