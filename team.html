// Staff code

Our promise

What you can expect from every member of the team.

Same rules for all

Staff play by the exact same rules. No spawned gear, no teleports, no favours.

Evidence first

Decisions are based on logs and video – not on who shouts loudest.

Fair appeals

Ban appeals are reviewed by a staff member who wasn't involved in the original decision.

Join the team

We're always looking for reliable moderators. You should be:

  • Active on the server and on Discord
  • Calm, fair and able to handle heated situations
  • Free of bans for at least 6 months
  • Comfortable reviewing clips and logs
