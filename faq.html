Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Our team is on Discord.

Getting started

How do I join the server?

Search for “Project Blackout” in the DayZ Launcher or the DZSA Launcher, or use Direct connect with . The join guide walks you through every step.

Do I need mods?

Any required mods are listed on the server info page. The DZSA Launcher downloads and loads them automatically, so you don't have to install anything by hand.

The server doesn't show up / I can't connect.

Clear your server browser filters or use Direct connect. For mod, BattlEye and version errors, see troubleshooting.

Is the server first-person or third-person?

See the current settings on the server info page.

Gameplay

When does the server restart?

Several times a day on a fixed schedule. The server info page shows the full schedule and a live countdown to the next restart. Log out somewhere safe before it hits.

When is the next wipe?

Wipes are announced on our Discord at least one week in advance. There are no surprise wipes.

How big can my group be?

Groups can have up to 6 players, and teaming with other groups isn't allowed. See rules – section 4.

Can I raid offline bases?

Yes. Raiding is allowed at any time, as long as you use legitimate in-game means. See rules – section 6.

I lost my gear to a bug or desync. Will it be replaced?

Generally no – DayZ is DayZ. Exceptions are only made for proven server-side issues with video evidence. Open a bug ticket if you think that's the case.

Rules & bans

How do I report a cheater or rule breaker?

Open a ticket on our Discord and include video evidence. Use the report template – it has everything staff need.

I was banned. How do I appeal?

Open a ban appeal ticket on Discord using the appeal template. Appeals are reviewed by a staff member who wasn't involved in the ban.

Is combat logging really bannable?

Yes. Logging out during or right after a fight to save your character is one of the most punished offences on the server. See rule 3.2.

Can I stream or record on the server?

Absolutely – content creators are welcome. Stream sniping, however, is against the rules.

Community

How do I find a squad?

Join our Discord and introduce yourself – plenty of players are looking for teammates.

Can I join the staff team?

When we're recruiting, applications open on Discord. Check the requirements on the team page.

How can I support the server?

Play fair, bring your friends and be active in the community. That's what keeps a server alive.

Our team answers on Discord – usually within a day.
