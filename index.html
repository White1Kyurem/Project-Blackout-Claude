// 04 — Rulebook

The Rules

Hard fights need fair rules. By joining the server, you accept them.

General
  1. Be respectful – no insults, no racism, no discrimination.
  2. Cheats, hacks, macros and third-party tools are prohibited.
  3. Report bugs and glitches – don't exploit them.
  4. Admin decisions are final.
PvP
  1. Kill on sight is allowed – everywhere on the map.
  2. No combat logging: logging out mid-fight can get you banned.
  3. No ghosting – don't relog to flank your enemies.
  4. No deliberate spawn camping.
Base Building
  1. No bases in military zones, large cities or at airdrop locations.
  2. No glitch bases – nothing inside rocks, trees or building walls.
  3. Every base must be raidable without glitches.
  4. Roads and bridges must not be blocked.
Raids
  1. Raiding is allowed – only with legitimate means such as tools and explosives.
  2. No raiding through glitches, walls or map exploits.
  3. Destroyed bases will not be restored by admins.
  4. Suspect a rule violation? Save the clip and report it on Discord.

You'll find the complete and up-to-date rulebook on our Discord. Violations can result in a kick or ban.
