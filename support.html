// 01 — Tickets

How to open a ticket

  1. Join our Discord

    All support runs through Discord – we don't handle reports in game chat.

  2. Open a ticket

    Go to the support channel and create a ticket for your topic.

  3. Paste the template

    Copy the matching template below, fill it in and attach your evidence.

  4. Wait for staff

    We aim to respond within 24 hours. Please don't ping or DM staff about your ticket.

Open Discord

Good evidence

  • Record with a replay buffer (NVIDIA ShadowPlay, AMD ReLive, OBS or Medal) so you never miss a moment.
  • Keep clips unedited and include at least 30 seconds before the incident.
  • Note the server time – it makes finding the right logs much faster.
  • Upload long videos to YouTube (unlisted) and share the link.
