The Rulebook

Hard fights need fair rules. By joining the server or our Discord, you accept everything on this page – not knowing a rule doesn't protect you from its penalty.

01 General

  1. 1.1

    Respect other players and staff. Trash talk about the game is fine – racism, sexism, threats and any form of discrimination are not, whether in-game, in voice chat or on Discord.

  2. 1.2

    One account per player. Alt accounts used to scout, hold loot or evade a ban are prohibited.

  3. 1.3

    Your in-game name must not be offensive and must not impersonate staff or other players.

  4. 1.4

    Staff decisions are final in-game. If you disagree, open a ticket on Discord – don't argue in chat.

  5. 1.5

    Lost gear is not refunded. Exceptions are only made for proven server-side issues with video evidence.

02 Communication

  1. 2.1

    No spam, advertising or links to other servers in text chat or on Discord.

  2. 2.2

    Hate speech and real-life threats result in an immediate permanent ban.

  3. 2.3

    Never share other players' personal information (doxxing).

  4. 2.4

    No soundboards or deliberately loud audio spam in proximity voice chat.

  5. 2.5

    English is the main language in global text chat, so staff can moderate it.

03 PvP & Combat

  1. 3.1

    Kill on sight (KOS) is allowed everywhere on the map.

  2. 3.2

    No combat logging. Don't log out during a fight or within 5 minutes after one to save your character. Alt+F4 and pulling your network cable count as combat logging.

  3. 3.3

    No ghosting. Don't relog or switch servers to gain a position on players you are fighting or raiding. Wait at least 15 minutes and log back in at least 500 m away.

  4. 3.4

    No deliberate spawn camping – repeatedly killing fresh spawns at the coast is not allowed.

  5. 3.5

    No stream sniping. Using a live stream to locate or ambush a player is prohibited.

04 Groups & Clans

  1. 4.1

    The maximum group size is 6 players – including anyone you actively cooperate with.

  2. 4.2

    No teaming beyond the group limit. Alliances between groups are prohibited.

  3. 4.3

    No rotating members to get around the limit. It applies at all times, not just per session.

  4. 4.4

    Clan names and tags must not be offensive.

05 Base Building

  1. 5.1

    No building within 1,000 m of military zones, airfields or major cities.

  2. 5.2

    No building within 500 m of airdrop locations, event zones or player spawns.

  3. 5.3

    No glitch bases. Walls, gates or containers inside rocks, trees, terrain or map buildings are not allowed.

  4. 5.4

    Every base must be accessible and raidable by legit means.

  5. 5.5

    Don't block roads, bridges, loot spawns or the entrances of map buildings.

  6. 5.6

    One main base per group. Bases that break these rules can be removed without warning.

06 Raiding

  1. 6.1

    Raiding is allowed at any time.

  2. 6.2

    Raid only with legitimate in-game means such as tools, explosives and lockpicks.

  3. 6.3

    No glitching through walls, gates, doors or terrain to get inside a base.

  4. 6.4

    Don't log out inside another group's base.

  5. 6.5

    Destroyed bases and stolen loot are not restored by staff.

07 Vehicles

  1. 7.1

    Don't hide vehicles inside walls, rocks or terrain.

  2. 7.2

    Don't use vehicles to block roads, bridges or spawn areas.

  3. 7.3

    Vehicle physics glitches (flying, wall-clipping) must not be abused – report them instead.

  4. 7.4

    Vehicles lost to desync or bugs are not replaced.

08 Cheats & Exploits

  1. 8.1

    Cheats, hacks, scripts, macros and any third-party software that gives an unfair advantage are strictly prohibited.

  2. 8.2

    Exploiting bugs is prohibited – this includes duping, wall glitching and getting under the map.

  3. 8.3

    Modified game files, custom textures or settings that remove grass, fog or darkness are prohibited.

  4. 8.4

    Knowingly playing with a cheater – or benefiting from their loot – is punished like cheating.

  5. 8.5

    Found a bug? Report it to staff. Players who report exploits responsibly won't be punished.

09 Staff & Reports

  1. 9.1

    Report rule breaks through a Discord ticket – with video evidence wherever possible.

  2. 9.2

    False reports and edited evidence are punished.

  3. 9.3

    Don't ask staff for items, teleports or refunds, and don't DM staff for support – use tickets.

  4. 9.4

    Staff play by the same rules as everyone else and may spectate players to investigate reports.
